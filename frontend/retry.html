💡 Improvement Tips for Next Attempt

🪞 Mirror Check Habit
Before every turn and lane change — check left mirror, right mirror, rear mirror. Make it a reflex, not a thought.
🏔️ H-Point Practice
Practice on a slope daily. Stop the vehicle, hold clutch + brake, then release slowly without rolling back. Repeat 20 times.
🔄 Figure-8 Technique
Keep speed very slow (walking speed). Look ahead at where you want to go, not at the ground. Wide turns are safer.
😌 Control Anxiety
Take 3 deep breaths before starting. Tell yourself: "I know how to drive." Slow = safe. Inspector prefers slow and careful.
⚡ Speed Control
Keep speed between 20–30 kmph throughout the test track. Never accelerate at turns. Brake early and smoothly.
🚦 Signal Discipline
Give indicators 30 meters before every turn. Don't jump stop lines. Wait for green even if road is clear.

📅 Your 7-Day Practice Plan

Day 1
Rest + Review — Watch your test failure areas on YouTube. Understand exactly what the inspector checks.
Day 2
Mirror Drills — Practice driving in a parking lot, checking mirrors every 10 seconds. Make it automatic.
Day 3
Slope Practice — Find a gentle slope. Practice stopping and restarting 20+ times without rolling back.
Day 4
Figure-8 + Slow Speed — Practice tight turns at walking speed. Stay in your lane. No foot on ground (2W).
Day 5
Full Mock Test — Do one complete simulation of the test track from start to finish. Time yourself.
Day 6
Mental Preparation — Read the pass/fail rules. Visualize clearing the test successfully. Sleep well tonight.
Day 7
Test Day! — Arrive 30 mins early. Carry all documents. Breathe. You've got this! 🚀
