📋 How to Apply for Learner License (LL)

A complete step-by-step guide to get your Learner License — the first step towards your Permanent DL

🪪 What is a Learner License?

A Learner License (LL) is a temporary driving permit issued by the RTO that allows you to practice driving under supervision before applying for a Permanent Driving License. It is valid for 6 months (180 days) from the date of issue. You must wait at least 30 days after getting your LL before applying for the Permanent DL test.

🗺️ Step-by-Step Process to Get LL
  • Check Eligibility Make sure you meet the age requirement: 16+ years for 2-wheeler, 18+ years for 4-wheeler and commercial vehicles. You must be a citizen of India or have valid residency proof. ⏱ Time: 2 minutes
  • Go to Parivahan Portal (Online — Recommended) Visit parivahan.gov.in → Click "Driving License Related Services" → Select your State → Click "Apply for Learner License". ⏱ Time: 10 minutes
  • Fill Application Form (Form 2) Enter your personal details: full name, date of birth, address, vehicle category. Upload scanned documents (Aadhaar, photo, signature). Verify mobile number via OTP. ⏱ Time: 15 minutes
  • Book Appointment Slot Select your nearest RTO from the list. Choose an available date and time slot for the theory test. Note down your Application Number — you will need it at the RTO. ⏱ Time: 5 minutes
  • Pay Application Fee Online Pay the LL application fee online via Net Banking, UPI, or Debit/Credit Card. Save the payment receipt. Fee is approximately ₹200–₹300 depending on your state. ⏱ Time: 5 minutes
  • Visit RTO on Appointment Day Arrive 15 minutes before your slot. Carry all original documents + photocopies. Submit documents at the counter. Get your token number for the theory test. 🏛️ Visit RTO on your booked date
  • Clear the Theory Test (Computer Based) The LL theory test is a 15-question computer-based MCQ test on traffic signs, rules, and road safety. You need 9/15 correct answers to pass (60% passing marks). Duration: 15 minutes. 📝 Test duration: 15 minutes
  • Collect Your Learner License If you pass, your LL is issued the same day. Some states send it by post within 7 days. Your LL card shows: your name, photo, LL number, issue date, and expiry date. ✅ LL issued same day or by post

Road Signs
Mandatory, cautionary, informatory signs
📏
Speed Limits

🛑
Driving Rules
Overtaking, lane discipline, parking
🚑
Emergency Rules
Ambulance right of way, accident protocol
⚖️
Motor Vehicle Act
Penalties, drunk driving, seatbelt rules
✅ Passing mark: 9/15 (60%) — Study traffic signs especially, they are most common in the test.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Can I apply for LL online without visiting RTO? ▼
Yes! You can complete the entire application online on parivahan.gov.in including document upload, fee payment, and slot booking. You only need to visit RTO for the theory test and document verification.
What if I fail the LL theory test? ▼
Don't worry! You can retake the test after 7 days. You will need to pay the test fee again. Most states allow unlimited attempts. Study traffic signs and Motor Vehicle Act rules carefully before retrying.
How long is the LL valid? ▼
Your Learner License is valid for 6 months (180 days) from the date of issue. You must apply for your Permanent DL within this period. If it expires, you must apply for a fresh LL from scratch.
Can I drive alone with a Learner License? ▼
No! A Learner License holder MUST be accompanied by a person who holds a valid Permanent Driving License. You must also display an "L" board (red L on white background) on your vehicle. Driving alone with LL is illegal.
What is the minimum age for LL? ▼
For 2-wheelers up to 50cc: 16 years. For all other vehicles (2W above 50cc, 4W, commercial): 18 years. For commercial vehicles: 18 years with additional requirements.
