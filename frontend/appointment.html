📦 What to Carry on Test Day
  • ✅ Original Learner License (LL)
  • ✅ 2 Photocopies of LL
  • ✅ Original Age Proof (Aadhaar / Birth Certificate)
  • ✅ Original Address Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID)
  • ✅ Filled Form 9 (Application for DL)
  • ✅ 2 Recent Passport-size Photographs
  • ✅ RTO Test Fee — ₹300 approx (cash or DD)
  • ✅ Payment receipt from your LL application
  • ✅ Medical Certificate Form 1A (age above 40)
💡 Carry both originals and photocopies of every document. Missing even one can disqualify you.
⏰ Test Day — Hour by Hour Plan
  • Reach RTO Office7:30 AM
    Arrive 30 minutes before your slot. Find the DL Test counter and submit your documents to the clerk.
  • Document Verification8:00 AM
    Officer checks your LL, Form 9, age/address proof and photographs. You receive a token number for your test slot.
  • Fee Payment8:30 AM
    Pay the driving test fee at the cash counter. Keep the receipt safely — it is required for DL collection later.
  • Wait for Your Token9:00 AM
    Wait in the designated area. Tests are called by token number. Average wait time: 30–60 minutes depending on footfall.
  • Driving Test Begins10:00 AM
    Inspector calls your token. Bring your vehicle to the test track. Test duration: 5–10 minutes per candidate.
  • Result Announcement11:00 AM
    Inspector marks Pass/Fail on your form. If passed, submit remaining documents. If failed, you can retry after 7 days.
  • DL DispatchWithin 30 days
    Your Permanent DL is dispatched to your registered address by Speed Post. Track on parivahan.gov.in using your application number.
🛣️ How the Test Track Works
Standard RTO test track path (may vary slightly by RTO):
🚦
Start Point — Inspector signals you to begin. Check mirrors, wear seatbelt/helmet BEFORE starting engine.
➡️
Straight Line Drive — Drive straight at controlled speed (20–30 kmph). No swerving or lane crossing.
🔄
Left Turn — Signal left, check mirror, slow down, take wide turn. Stop completely at stop line before proceeding.
⛰️
H-Point / Slope Start (4W) — Stop on slope, apply handbrake. Start without rolling back more than 30cm.
🔢
Figure-8 (2W) — Ride figure-8 pattern at slow speed. Both feet must stay on footrests throughout.
🅿️
Parking (4W) — Parallel park or reverse park in the marked bay within 3 attempts maximum.
🏁
End Point — Stop at marked finish line. Apply handbrake. Turn off engine. Wait for inspector's verdict.
🎯 Key Disciplines Inspector Will Check
🪞 Mirror UsageCheck rear and side mirrors before every turn, lane change, and stop. Inspector watches your eye movement.
🚦 Signal UsageIndicate left/right at least 30 metres before turning. Never turn without signalling first.
⚖️ Speed ControlMaintain 20–30 kmph on test track. No sudden acceleration or harsh braking at any point.
🛑 Stop LinesCome to a complete stop at every stop line. Rolling stops are marked as failures automatically.
🦺 Safety GearHelmet mandatory for 2W. Seatbelt mandatory for 4W. Must be worn BEFORE starting the engine.
👀 Road AwarenessLook ahead, not at the ground. Watch for inspector's hand signals throughout the test.
🔄 Gear Shifting (4W)Shift gears smoothly without stalling. No grinding. Use clutch properly before every gear change.
📏 Lane DisciplineStay in your designated lane. Do not cross marked lines. Keep safe distance from cones/markers.
✅ Do's and ❌ Don'ts

✅ DO These

  • Greet the inspector politely
  • Adjust mirrors before starting engine
  • Wear helmet / seatbelt FIRST
  • Stop completely at all stop lines
  • Signal before every turn
  • Drive at steady controlled speed
  • Stay calm — take deep breaths
  • Ask inspector politely if unclear

❌ DON'T Do These

  • Don't use mobile phone on track
  • Don't skip mirror checks
  • Don't roll back on slope (4W)
  • Don't put feet down on 2W
  • Don't brake suddenly or harshly
  • Don't argue with inspector
  • Don't stall engine 3+ times
  • Don't cross any stop lines
